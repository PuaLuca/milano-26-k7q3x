Day 4

Sunday  ·  22 November 2026
Make your move.

Free day

Free day for everyone. Here are some ideas to enjoy Milano your own way.

Iconic landmarks

Duomo di Milano: inside and up to the rooftop
Visit the cathedral, then climb to the terraces by stairs or lift. Skip-the-line tickets available.
Castello Sforzesco
Sforza Castle, in the centre of Milan.
Teatro alla Scala
Milan’s world-famous opera house.

Art

The Last Supper
Leonardo da Vinci’s fresco at Santa Maria delle Grazie. Book well ahead.
Pinacoteca di Brera
The picture gallery of the Brera district.
Museo del Novecento
20th-century art, on Piazza del Duomo.
Fondazione Prada
Contemporary art and culture.

Design and science

ADI Design Museum
Italian design.
National Science and Technology Museum
Named after Leonardo da Vinci.

More ideas

Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II
The 19th-century glass-and-iron shopping arcade next to the Duomo.
Duomo Museum
The cathedral’s own museum, inside Palazzo Reale on Piazza del Duomo.
Piazza dei Mercanti
A medieval square between the Duomo and Piazza Cordusio, with its “whispering gallery”.
San Siro stadium and museum tour
Home of AC Milan and Inter. The tour can change or stop on match days.
Lake Como
About an hour by train from Milan. Link: lake ferry timetables.

Good to know

Book tickets online in advance for popular spots such as the Duomo and The Last Supper.
Check Sunday opening hours on each website before you go.
